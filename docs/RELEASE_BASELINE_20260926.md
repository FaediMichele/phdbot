# Essential release target: 3 October 2026

User-approved scope on 26 September: aim for a basically reliable release in
about one calendar week. This is a planning target, conditional on governor
availability, not a certified completion estimate or exhaustive source coverage.

## Required release evidence

- Preserve existing university search and expand collection to research centres,
  institutes and foundations through the existing registry and scheduler.
- Demonstrate complete official-source -> extraction -> quality -> live-search
  paths on representative institution types and reusable portal families.
- Audit at least 20 current records across universities, institutes and foundations
  and at least four countries, including negative/expired controls. Record the
  actual sample and errors; this small sample is not a statistical accuracy claim.
- Resolve known consequential errors in the release sample: wrong opportunity
  identity/type, unsupported deadline/funding, duplicates and inaccessible detail.
  Unverified facts remain explicit uncertainty; abstention is acceptable.
- Verify feedback hides exact items; failed sources do not block productive peers;
  completed run IDs and safe restart semantics remain intact.
- Full unit suite, Ruff, mypy, live search canaries and documented recovery before
  handoff. Record both useful results and technical failures, not just row counts.

## Current useful block

BITE reads the public response emitted by a single official embedded board,
retaining the publisher's language/query filters. It rejects changed widgets,
redirects, missing responses and truncated feeds (over 1000 or incomplete rows).
Portal display windows and search keywords are not application facts. The live
psychiatry sample returned two public details with zero schema-model calls.

The group-leader detail states application deadline 12 October 2026 and reference
letters 19 October. Current deterministic extraction abstains; do not substitute
API endsOn=19 October. Deadline parsing repair is separate from adapter evaluation.
Neither this senior role nor a title containing research IT implies a PhD place.

## Scope control

Time-box a new exceptional portal investigation to roughly 30 minutes of active
engineering, then retain evidence and defer unless it blocks a required release
property or affects a common family. Prefer cohort evidence and reusable fixes.
Do not reopen completed Umantis work or rerun passed checks without a change.

After release: rare ATS variants, exhaustive registry activation, isolated broken
sites, optional compensation/group enrichment, additional performance tuning and
review of evidence-poor records. Adaptive refresh and architectural proposals
remain future work; they are not release blockers.
