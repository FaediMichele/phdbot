Support for developing education at Master’s and Doctoral levels is offered by the Foundation to students and scholars from the region (Lebanon, Cyprus, Syria and Egypt). This support is offered in two ways:

Targeted Scholarships: Requiring students from the region to conduct research in maritime archaeology at a selected institution with relevant academic expertise/programmes relating to maritime archaeology of the eastern Mediterranean.

Open Scholarships: Enabling students from the region to undertake research in maritime archaeology at a university of their choice.

General Requirements:

  • Must demonstrate a genuine interest in maritime archaeology of the eastern Mediterranean region and would be expected to develop the subject in their home country upon their return.
  • Required to write their Master’s dissertation or PhD thesis on a subject relevant to the HFF regional remit
  • Possess a good 2:1 honours degree (or equivalent) at an appropriate level in a relevant topic, preferably archaeology. For a PhD, this must be in either archaeology or a related discipline (preferably maritime archaeology).
  • You must be a national of Cyprus, Lebanon, Egypt or Syria.

Full Terms and Conditions for HFF Scholarships can be found here.

The deadline for all Masters and PhD scholarship applications to HFF is March 16th annually. Deadlines for Diplomas and Minors vary.

It is the applicant’s responsibility to ensure they meet the programme requirements and apply by the programme deadline.

Shortlisted candidates will be contacted by the end of April to arrange an interview. Final decisions on scholarships will be made by Mid-May.

For general enquiries and to discuss potential topics, please contract Dr. Dani Newman

American University of Beirut- Marine Sciences and Culture program

The Honor Frost Foundation offers an annual scholarship for qualified Lebanese University students and archaeologists, who are Lebanese nationals or permanent residents of Lebanon, to attend the Marine Sciences and Culture program at the American University of Beirut. Interested candidates are requested to submit applications to the program coordinator (see below).

Students will be registered at the American University of Beirut as visiting scholars, and provided with access to a variety of academic and electronic resources through the university. They will be given a general background in a variety of subjects related to maritime archaeology and geomorphology, and be supervised by a panel of experts from the HFF Lebanon Team and AUB. After successfully undertaking the program, students are awarded a certificate of completion.

The HFF students are required to complete four of the five MSCU courses detailed here. These include a fieldwork course that provides students with a hands-on experience in geophysical and diving survey techniques.

Entry Requirements:

The selection process will be conducted through a ranking system for applicants based on previous archaeological experience and dedication to the promotion and sustenance of archaeology in Lebanon. Experience in maritime archaeology projects is preferred but not required. There are no formal barriers to entry, and all interested persons are encouraged to apply.

Maintenance:

Successful applicants are expected to maintain at least a 72% average throughout the year, and to participate regularly in all courses, trainings and workshops associated with the program. They are also required to submit full reports at the end of each semester, along with a web profile for the HFF website.

Application Procedure:

The deadline for applications is 31st March annually. The application should be in English and include the following:
  • Curriculum Vitae
  • Cover letter (up to 500 words) further detailing the candidate’s qualifications and the relevance of their application to the scholarship and program
  • A reference letter from an academic advisor or field archaeologists, preferably with a maritime background

The scholarship will cover all costs of the MSCU program directly with AUB, and covers all associated expenses for students. Interested candidates should submit their application to the program coordinator via email:

Open Scholarships

This scholarship is available to nationals of Cyprus, Lebanon, Egypt, and Syria (only). While this scholarship may be used at any university, it is the responsibility of the applicant to justify the selection of that institution. It is the applicant’s responsibility to have applied for the programme before applying to The Foundation.

Funding and Stipends

UK Programmes (1 year): £19,500 with an additional travel fund of £1500, which can be drawn as required during your study. Tuition fees will also be paid directly to the University at the appropriate fee rate.

Non-UK Programmes: You will be required to submit a detailed budget with your application, including a stipend, visa & insurance fees & all academic fees. This stipend must not exceed any full scholarships offered by the University in the faculty in which you are studying.

Open Scholarships

This scholarship is available to nationals of Cyprus, Lebanon, Egypt, and Syria (only). While this scholarship may be used at any university, it is the responsibility of the applicant to justify the selection of that institution. It is the applicant’s responsibility to have applied for the programme before applying to The Foundation.

While university choice is open, students are expected to be supervised by experts in maritime archaeology. It is expected that students complete their degrees on-site, not via distance learning. HFF recognises that time away from the university may be required for essential fieldwork or thesis-related training. 

Funding and Stipends

UK Programmes (3 years): £19,500 per annum with an additional travel fund of £1500, which can be drawn as required during your study. Tuition fees will also be paid directly to the University at the appropriate fee rate.

Non-UK Programmes: You will be required to submit a detailed budget with your application, including a stipend, visa & insurance fees & all academic fees. This stipend must not exceed any full scholarships offered by the University in the faculty in which you are studying.

Targeted Scholarships

The HFF offers a scholarship to support one candidate pursuing a PhD at the Department of History and Archaeology of the University of Cyprus (programme Archaeology of the Mediterranean from Prehistory to the Byzantine Era ) on a topic related to Coastal Archaeology of the Eastern Mediterranean.

Proposed doctoral research may focus on themes such as maritime geoarchaeology, digital approaches to the management of maritime cultural heritage, the archaeology of harbours and anchorages, and the assessment of coastal threats — including the impacts of climate change on coastal archaeology.

Eligibility for the scholarship is contingent upon successful admission to the PhD programme. You must apply through both the HFF scholarship program and the University of Cyprus. Please be aware that these programmes have different deadlines.

Admission: Fall Semester, Academic Year 2026/2027 (September 2026)

Application deadline for HFF scholarship: 16 March 2026 (apply here)

Application deadline to the University of Cyprus: 31 March 2026 (Apply here)
