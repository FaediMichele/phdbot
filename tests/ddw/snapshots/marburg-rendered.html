Open positions

Currently, the Max Planck Institute for Terrestrial Microbiology employs approximately 200 staff members and approximately 125 visiting researchers in basic research.
The administrative and support units assist the research divisions in carrying out scientific projects.

In addition to the job openings listed below, the Max Planck Institute for Terrestrial Microbiology invites applications from interested students, PhD students and postdoctoral fellows. Interested applicants are asked to contact the relevant director or group leader directly.
